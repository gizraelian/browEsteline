Permanent Brows

Four approaches from the original Brow Esteline eyebrow collection, each with a distinct finish.

Ombre Brows

A semi-permanent shading technique intended to create a soft pencil-like finish and more defined-looking brows.

  • Suited to a defined, softly shaded look
  • Original site describes a result lasting up to two years

Nano / 3D Hair Strokes

A machine technique—not microblading—used to create hair-like strokes and fill visible gaps.

  • Designed for a natural-looking result
  • Original site describes a result lasting up to 1.5 years

Combo Brows

A mix of nano hair strokes and ombre shading for a natural-looking front and defined shape.

  • Combines two existing techniques
  • Original site describes a result lasting up to two years

Lamination & Tint

A hair treatment that styles brows into the desired shape and adds tint. It may be combined with a lash lift.

  • Styles and defines brow hair
  • Original site describes a result lasting up to three months
