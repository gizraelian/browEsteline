Your privacy is a priority. Contact Brow Esteline directly to discuss the treatment area; this website does not collect treatment details.

Preparation

Before and between sessions

Before

  • Shave 24 hours before the appointment
  • Arrive with clean skin and no cream or deodorant on the treated area
  • Services are by appointment

Between sessions

  • Do not wax, pluck or thread the hair
  • Allow four to five weeks between sessions
  • Packages include six sessions
