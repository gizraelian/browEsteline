How it works

Targeting pigment in the hair

The existing page explains that laser hair removal uses specific wavelengths of light to target melanin in the hair, warming the hair and root.

Treatment suitability, expected results, contraindications and safety guidance must be discussed directly with the practitioner. This page does not offer medical advice.

Preparation

How to prepare

  • Shave 24 hours before the procedure
  • Do not wax, tweeze or sugar the area
  • Do not apply cream, deodorant or makeup on the treatment area
  • The existing page advises no sauna or sunbathing for 24 hours after treatment
  • Current packages include six sessions
  • The existing page specifies four to five weeks between sessions
