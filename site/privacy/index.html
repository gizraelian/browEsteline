Privacy & Tracking

This website is designed to remain fully usable without optional marketing cookies.

Essential site operation

The website works without marketing cookies. Basic features such as navigation, phone links, WhatsApp links and the embedded map do not depend on marketing tracking.

Optional marketing analytics

If optional analytics are offered, they load only after a visitor chooses to allow them. Declining does not limit access to the website.

Events

Optional analytics are limited to general website activity such as page views and clicks on phone, email, WhatsApp or booking links. A booking-link click is not treated as a completed booking. Treatment details, form content, health information and appointment information are not sent.

Your choice

If a privacy choice is shown, the selection is stored in the browser. Clearing site data resets that choice.

Contact

Questions about this website's privacy or tracking practices can be sent to BrowEsteline@gmail.com.
