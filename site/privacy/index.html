Privacy-conscious by default

Privacy & Tracking

Marketing tracking is disabled until real platform IDs are supplied and the business owner approves the final wording.

Essential site operation

The website works without marketing cookies. Basic features such as navigation, phone links, WhatsApp links and the embedded map do not depend on marketing tracking.

Optional marketing analytics

If enabled in the future, the site can load Meta Pixel and Google Analytics only after the configured consent condition is met. A visitor who declines can still use the full website.

Events

Prepared events are limited to generic actions such as page views, phone clicks, contact actions, WhatsApp actions and booking-link clicks. A click is not recorded as a completed booking. Treatment details, form content, health information and appointment information must never be transmitted.

Your choice

The choice is stored locally in the browser under esteline-marketing-consent. Clearing site data resets it. This wording must be reviewed by Katherine or her legal adviser before marketing tracking is enabled.

Contact

Questions about this website's privacy or tracking practices can be sent to BrowEsteline@gmail.com.
