How laser hair removal works?

Hair removal lasers target melanin pigment in the hair with specific wavelengths of light energy.

The energy heats the hair quickly and powerfully then the heat spreads to the hair root, destroying it.

How to prepare for session?

  • Do not wax, tweeze or sugar the area
  • No cream, deodorant or makeup on the area of treatment
  • No sauna or sunbathing 24 after the treatment
